# Combine several images

Prompts that use two or more uploaded images at once: people together in one photo, a product in a room, a face with an outfit, and collages. Always say which image is which.

## 1. Two people at a cafe table

Tags: two people, cafe, merge

```text
Put the woman from the first image and the man from the second image together in one photo at a cafe table, both laughing, lit by the same window light.
```

## 2. Product into a room

Tags: product, interior, placement

