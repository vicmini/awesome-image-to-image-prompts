# Pets

Prompts for pet photos: portraits, pets as humans, costumes and new scenes. Most of them end by keeping the animal's face and markings, since that is what makes it your pet.

## 1. Dog as a human

Tags: pet as human, dog, portrait

```text
Show this dog as a human with the same coloring and personality: a man in his twenties with sandy hair and a brown jacket, standing in the same park.
```
