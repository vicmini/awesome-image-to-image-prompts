# Remove, add and expand

Prompts for taking things out of a photo, putting new things in, and extending the frame beyond its original edges. Each one names the change and what must stay untouched.

## 1. Remove background people

Tags: remove, people, background

```text
Remove the people in the background. Fill the space with what would naturally be behind them. Keep the main subject unchanged.
```

## 2. Remove power lines and trash can

Tags: remove, cleanup, outdoor

```text
Remove the power lines and the trash can from this photo.
```

## 3. Add a dog on the bench

Tags: add, animal, lighting

```text
Add a golden retriever sitting next to him on the bench, lit by the same afternoon light.
```

## 4. Expand to a wide frame

Tags: expand, outpainting, landscape

```text
Expand this photo to a wide 16:9 frame, continuing the landscape on both sides naturally.
```

## 5. Clear a tourist crowd from a landmark

Tags: remove, travel, people

```text
Remove every tourist standing in front of the old stone cathedral and rebuild the empty plaza tiles and steps behind them. Keep the cathedral, the sky and the morning shadows exactly as they are.
```

## 6. Remove a photobomber from a beach shot

Tags: remove, people, beach

```text
Remove the man in the red shirt walking behind the couple and continue the sand and waves where he stood. Keep the couple, their pose and the sunset light unchanged.
```

## 7. Remove the photographer's shadow

Tags: remove, shadow, outdoor

```text

```

## 8. Remove a car from a driveway
