# Change lighting, weather and season

Prompts that change the light, the time of day, the weather or the season in a photo while the scene itself stays put. Light direction and color temperature do most of the work, so each prompt names them.

## 1. Midday to golden hour

Tags: golden hour, time of day, outdoor

```text
Change the harsh midday light in this street portrait to golden hour, with low warm sun from the right, long shadows and a soft glow on her hair. Keep her face, pose and the buildings unchanged.
```

## 2. Day to blue hour

Tags: blue hour, city, time of day

```text

```

## 3. Day to night with streetlights

Tags: night, street, time of day

