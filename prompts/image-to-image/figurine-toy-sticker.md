# Figurines, toys and stickers

Prompts that turn a person, pet or object into a collectible figure, toy, plush or sticker set. They work best with a clear photo where the face and outfit are easy to see.

## 1. Collectible figure on a desk

Tags: figurine, collectible, desk

```text
Turn this person into a 1/7 scale collectible figure standing on a round clear base on a desk. Behind it, put the figure's printed box with their name, and a monitor showing the 3D model.
```

