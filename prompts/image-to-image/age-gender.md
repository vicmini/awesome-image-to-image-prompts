# Age and gender

Prompts for showing a person older, younger or as another gender. Good gender swaps change the bone structure like a sibling would have, not just the hair and clothes.

## 1. Opposite gender like a sibling

Tags: gender swap, face, sibling

```text
Show this person as the opposite gender, like a sibling: change the jaw, brow, nose and face shape, not just the hair and clothes. Keep the eye color, skin tone, pose and background.
```

## 2. Male to female

Tags: gender swap, male to female, face

```text

```
